1. About this app

Dream IPTV Player is a media player. It does not provide IPTV subscriptions, television channels, movies, or other content. Users connect to IPTV services they choose independently.

2. Information processed

When you sign in, the app processes the IPTV server address, username, and password you enter to authenticate with your IPTV provider and retrieve available content. The app stores connection details on your device using Android application preferences so that you can reconnect. App preferences, such as selected language, and the start time of the two-hour free trial are also stored on your device.

3. IPTV provider connections

To access IPTV content, the app sends your connection information to the IPTV server you specify. The security of that connection depends on the server URL and configuration: an HTTP connection is not encrypted, while HTTPS can provide transport encryption. The IPTV provider operates independently and may process data under its own privacy policy. Do not enter credentials for services you do not trust.

4. Payments

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details under its own policies. The app receives purchase and entitlement information needed to enable paid features; it does not need to collect your full payment card number.

5. Data retention and deletion

Locally saved IPTV login details can be removed by signing out or clearing the app's data. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data, subject to Android backup and device behavior. The free-trial state is stored locally. To request information about data handled by an independent IPTV provider, contact that provider directly. Google Play purchase history is managed by Google.

6. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children. Content availability is controlled by the IPTV provider chosen by the user.

7. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
